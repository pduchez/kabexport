Nimlajacoc

Cardamom from the forest floor

In Nimlajacoc, cardamom grows beneath native trees, is picked by hand and is dried in the producers' own facility. This page shows what we have seen there with our own eyes, and marks clearly what we are still documenting.

What we can show you

Every claim, with its evidence

Each statement on this page carries a status. Seen on site means we saw it ourselves on a visit, and the photo is from there. Being documented means we know it, but the proof is not published yet.

Seen on site

Grown under native forest shade

Cardamom grows in the understory, beneath tall native trees. We walked the plots on our visit.

Seen on site

Picked by hand, plant by plant

Harvesters work plant by plant, picking ripe capsules from the panicles at the base of each plant.

Seen on site

Ripeness checked in the field

A ripe capsule holds dark seeds; a pale one is still young. Opening a few capsules tells the harvester whether a plant is ready.

Seen on site

Dried at origin, in the producers' own facility

Fresh capsules are dried in heated drying bins inside the producers' own facility in the community.

Seen on site

Organic product handled apart

The drying bins and the processing area are labelled for organic product, so organic lots are handled on their own.

Seen on site

Every sack weighed

Sacks are weighed one by one on a digital hanging scale inside the facility.

Being documented

Organic certificate

The producers hold third-party organic certification. We will publish a copy of the current certificate here.

Being documented

Producers, plots and capacity

We are documenting each producing family, the size of their plots and what they harvest, with their consent.

From plant to sack

How cardamom is made in Nimlajacoc

Swipe to follow the process →

  1. 01

    Shade

    Native trees keep the soil cool and moist. Cardamom grows beneath them.

  2. 02

    Flower

    Each capsule starts as a small white flower veined in purple, close to the ground.

  3. 03

    Capsules

    The panicles run along the ground, heavy with green capsules.

  4. 04

    Harvest

    Picked by hand into woven bags, plant by plant.

  5. 05

    Ripeness

    Dark seeds mean a ripe capsule. Pale seeds mean wait.

  6. 06

    Drying

    Fresh capsules go into heated drying bins at the community's facility.

  7. 07

    Dried

    Once dry, the capsules turn firm and olive green, ready to be sorted and graded.

  8. 08

    Weighed

    Each sack goes on the scale before it is stored.

  9. 09

    On the road

    Sacks leave the community by pickup, along rural roads.

The families

The people behind this cardamom

Being documented

We are documenting each producing family together with them. Profiles are published only with each family's consent, and each one will include:

  • Name, and how long they have grown cardamom
  • Size of their plots
  • Number of plants and how they are managed
  • What they harvest in a typical season
  • In their own words: what the crop means for their family
Our commitments

Clear prices agreed before harvest, paid on time. Purchases through the producers' own organizations. Drying, sorting and packing at origin. Volumes planned season after season.
