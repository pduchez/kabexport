About us

Central American origin, international standards.

KAB EXPORT is a Central American company that takes the region's organic products to the world's most demanding markets, through its own supply chain in every country where it operates.

Kaab
beehoneyearth
Our name

A Maya word for honey, and for the earth

We chose a Maya name because our products grow on the same land where that culture flourished. Kaab names the bee and its honey, and also the earth. To us it sums up what we do: take the best of the Central American land to the world.

Who we are

A regional company with its own operations

We operate across Central America and are expanding in Guatemala, El Salvador and Nicaragua. In each country we work directly with producers, collection, processing and logistics to keep every shipment efficient and reliable.

Our customers include importers, distributors and food manufacturers in Germany, Spain, the United States, Mexico and Central America itself.

We run quality systems at every level of the organisation, from the field to the office, and report results and progress to our partners and stakeholders.

Mission

Take the best of Central America to the world

Vision
