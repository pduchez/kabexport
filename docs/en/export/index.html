The process

Five steps for every order

  1. SampleYou tell us what you need and we send samples of the available lot with its data sheet.
  2. Quote and contractWe agree on price, volume, specification, Incoterm and shipping date.
  3. PreparationWe sort, clean and pack your lot, and test it before release.
  4. DocumentsWe prepare the organic, phytosanitary and origin certificates and the commercial documents.
  5. ShippingWe arrange transport and keep you updated through to the port of destination.

Trade terms

Incoterms we work with

We choose together the Incoterm that best fits your logistics.

IncotermWhat it meansA good fit if…
EXWWe hand over at our facilities.You have your own freight forwarder in the region.
FCAWe hand over, export-cleared, to your carrier.You consolidate cargo with other suppliers.
FOBWe deliver on board the vessel at the port of loading.You book ocean freight yourself.
CIF / CFRWe pay freight (and insurance, for CIF) to your port.You prefer to receive the cargo at your port of destination.
FAQ

Questions buyers often ask

Do you send samples?

Yes. Tell us the product, format and delivery address. Samples ship with their technical data sheet.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product and format. Tell us the volume you need and we will explain how we can supply it.

How long does an order take?

Lead time depends on the harvest, the format and the destination. We give you a shipping date when the quote is confirmed.

Can you pack under my brand?

Ask us. We assess custom packing and labelling depending on the product and volume.

Which documents will I receive?

Organic certificate, certificate of analysis, phytosanitary and origin certificates, commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading. If your country requires anything else, we prepare it.
