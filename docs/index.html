Producers first

We measure our work by how the producer families are doing, not only by what we sell.

Fair, clear pricesAgreed with producers before the harvest, so they know what their work is worth.
Stronger organizationsWe buy through cooperatives and community associations and help them grow, rather than going around them.
Value at originDrying, fermenting, sorting and packing happen in Alta Verapaz, where the jobs are needed.

Our portfolio

Six crops from one agroforestry landscape

Cardamom, allspice, black pepper, clove, cinnamon and cacao all grow among trees. Together they give families income across the year and keep the land covered.

Green cardamom

Elettaria cardamomum

Grown under the forest canopy by family farms. Alta Verapaz grows most of Guatemala's cardamom.

About our cardamom →

Allspice

Pimenta dioica

Native to Mesoamerica. Berries from tall trees, harvested once a year and dried in the sun.

About our allspice →

Black pepper

Piper nigrum

A vine grown on living trees inside diversified family plots.

About our pepper →

Clove

Syzygium aromaticum

Flower buds picked by hand just before they open, then dried.

About our clove →

Cinnamon

Cinnamomum

Inner bark peeled by hand and dried into quills by community growers.

Our name

In Maya, the word for honey also names the earth.

That is why we are called KAB. Our products grow on Maya land, in the communities of northern Alta Verapaz, and everything we do starts from respect for that land and the people who live on it.

How we work

From sample to container

A clear process, so every buyer knows where their order stands.

  1. SampleWe send lot samples with their technical data sheet.
  2. Quote and contractPrice, volume, Incoterm and shipping date, planned around the harvest.
  3. PreparationSorting, cleaning and packing in Alta Verapaz.
  4. DocumentsPhytosanitary and origin certificates, and organic certificates for certified lots.
  5. ShippingTracking through to delivery in Canada or the United States.
