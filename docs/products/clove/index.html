Clove

Syzygium aromaticum

The clove is the unopened flower bud of an evergreen tree. In northern Alta Verapaz it is grown by families as part of diversified plots, together with other trees and spices.

Picked by hand, bud by bud

Buds are picked just before they open, when they turn from green to pink, and dried until they reach their characteristic reddish-brown colour.

Picking cloves is careful, manual work. Paying fairly for it is what makes it worthwhile for the families who do it.

Like allspice and cinnamon, clove is a tree crop: it produces for many years while the land stays covered.

Common uses

  • Baking and spiced desserts
  • Spice blends and seasonings
  • Hams and processed meats
  • Mulled wine, teas and drinks
  • Clove oil extraction
