Green cardamom

Elettaria cardamomum

Guatemala is the world's leading cardamom exporter, and Alta Verapaz grows most of its crop. Ours comes from family farms in the north of the department, grown under the forest canopy and picked by hand.

A spice that keeps families on their land

Cardamom is an understory plant: it needs shade, humidity and soil rich in organic matter, so it is grown beneath the trees rather than in place of them.

Almost all of Guatemala's cardamom is exported, so the income of thousands of families depends on international prices they do not control. Buying directly, with prices agreed in advance, is how we try to make that income steadier.

Pods are picked ripe, dried under controlled conditions to keep their green colour and essential oils, then cleaned and graded by size and colour.

Common uses

  • Chai and tea blends
  • Cardamom coffee
  • Baking, especially Nordic-style
  • Spice blends, curry and garam masala
  • Essential oil and oleoresin extraction
