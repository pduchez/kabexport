We handle the whole chain in Guatemala, from collection to the port, and coordinate delivery to your warehouse in Canada or the United States. You deal with one supplier who knows every step.

The process

Five steps for every order

  1. SampleYou tell us what you need and we send samples of the available lot with its data sheet.
  2. Quote and contractWe agree on price, volume, specification, Incoterm and shipping date, in line with the harvest calendar.
  3. PreparationYour lot is dried, cleaned, sorted and packed in Alta Verapaz, and checked before release.
  4. Documents and permitsWe handle the export registration, phytosanitary and origin certificates, customs clearance and the commercial documents.
  5. Shipping and deliveryWe ship from the port that suits your destination and keep you updated through to delivery.

Permits and paperwork

We know the process on both sides

Exporting food products means meeting the rules of the country of origin and of the country of destination. We take care of everything in Guatemala and prepare what your customs broker needs at destination, so the product reaches your warehouse or business without surprises.

In Guatemala, we handle

  • Export registration and declaration through Guatemala's export single window
  • Phytosanitary certificate from the Ministry of Agriculture (MAGA)
  • Certificate of origin
  • Customs clearance and loading at the port
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and transport document
  • Organic certificate, for certified lots

At destination, we coordinate

  • United StatesDocuments for FDA Prior Notice and CBP entry. Processing facilities registered with the FDA, and support for your FSVP
  • CanadaDocuments for CBSA entry and for an importer holding a Safe Food for Canadians licence (CFIA)
  • Door deliveryFreight, insurance and customs broker coordinated to your warehouse (DAP)

Duties and import taxes are paid at destination by the importer of record, unless agreed otherwise in the contract.

Trade terms

Incoterms we work with

From pick-up in Guatemala to delivery at your door. We choose together the Incoterm that best fits your logistics.

IncotermWhat it meansA good fit if…
EXWWe hand over at the collection facility in Guatemala.You have your own freight forwarder in the region.
FCAWe hand over, export-cleared, to your carrier.You consolidate cargo with other suppliers.
FOBWe deliver on board the vessel at a Guatemalan port.You book ocean freight yourself.
CFR / CIFWe pay ocean freight (and insurance, for CIF) to your port.You prefer to receive the cargo at your port of destination.
DAPWe deliver to your warehouse or business. You clear import customs.You want the product at your door with one supplier to deal with.
Visit the origin

Come and meet the producers

We host buyers who want to see where their spices and cacao come from. We organize visits to the producing communities in northern Alta Verapaz, to their farms and to their drying and processing facilities, together with the producer organizations.

The best time to visit is during the harvest of the product you buy. We help you plan the trip from Guatemala City, including transport within the region.

A visit includes

  • Farm and plot visits

FAQ

Questions buyers often ask

Do you send samples?

Yes. Tell us the product, format and delivery address. Samples ship by air with their technical data sheet.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product. We sell wholesale, by the pallet and above, and confirm the minimum for each product in the quote. We do not sell retail quantities.

Can you deliver to my warehouse?

Yes. Under DAP we coordinate freight, insurance and your customs broker so the product reaches your warehouse or business. Import duties and taxes are paid by the importer of record unless the contract says otherwise.

Which port do you ship from?

From the Atlantic ports of Santo Tomás de Castilla and Puerto Barrios, or from Puerto Quetzal on the Pacific, depending on your destination. We pick the most efficient route for your order.

Is every product available all year?

No. Each crop has its own harvest season, and some, such as allspice, are harvested once a year. See the harvest calendar on our home page and book ahead.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the harvest, the product and the destination. Once the quote is confirmed we give you a shipping date and keep you updated until delivery.

Can I visit the producers?

Yes. We organize visits to the communities, farms and processing facilities in Alta Verapaz, together with the producer organizations. Write to us to plan your trip.

Are your products organic?

Some lots come from certified organic producers and others from uncertified agroecological farms. We state the certification status of every lot in the quote.

Can I get an independent lab analysis?

Yes. On request, we arrange pre-shipment analysis by a third-party laboratory of your choice or ours.

Which documents will I receive?

Technical data sheet, phytosanitary and origin certificates, commercial invoice, packing list and transport document, plus the organic certificate for certified lots. If your country or your company requires anything else, tell us.
