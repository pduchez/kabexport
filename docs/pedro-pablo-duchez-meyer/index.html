Pedro Pablo Duchez Meyer

Pedro Pablo Duchez Meyer is a Guatemalan entrepreneur and the director of KAB EXPORT, the company that connects producer communities in Alta Verapaz with spice and cacao buyers in Canada and the United States.

Profile

Director

Strategic management · Agribusiness · Supply chains · Business structuring

30years in strategic management
3industries: agribusiness, real estate and tourism
2degrees: Business Administration and a Master's in Innovation
“The people who grow a spice should be the first to benefit from it. Everything else we build starts there.”

Pedro Pablo comes from a family with several generations in agriculture. For three decades he has directed and structured companies in agribusiness, real estate and tourism in Guatemala and El Salvador, always with the same approach: patient capital, well-founded decisions and trust built over years, not in a single transaction.

He knows the spice business from the field. He runs an organic cardamom farm in El Salvador and has his own farm in Alta Verapaz. At KAB EXPORT he leads the supply chain at origin: the relationship with producer organizations, the planning of each harvest and the logistics to North America.

KAB EXPORT is part of DAConsortium, the business group for which he serves as Chief Revenue Architect.
