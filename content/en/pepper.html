Black pepper

Piper nigrum

Black pepper is a climbing vine. In northern Alta Verapaz families grow it on living trees inside diversified plots, alongside cacao, fruit trees and other spices.

{{icon:pepper}}

A vine that climbs the trees

Pepper needs something to climb, so growers train it on shade and fruit trees. The same plot keeps producing wood, fruit and other crops, and the soil stays covered.

The berries are picked when the spikes start to ripen and dried until they turn black and wrinkled.

For families, pepper is one more source of income in the year, which makes them less dependent on the price of a single crop.

Common uses

  • Table pepper and grinders
  • Spice blends and seasonings
  • Meat processing and charcuterie
  • Sauces, dressings and ready meals
  • Oleoresin extraction
