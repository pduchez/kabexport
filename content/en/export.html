The process

Five steps for every order

  1. SampleYou tell us what you need and we send samples of the available lot with its data sheet.
  2. Quote and contractWe agree on price, volume, specification, Incoterm and shipping date, in line with the harvest calendar.
  3. PreparationYour lot is dried, cleaned, sorted and packed in Alta Verapaz, and checked before release.
  4. DocumentsWe prepare the phytosanitary and origin certificates, the commercial documents and, for certified lots, the organic certificate.
  5. ShippingWe arrange transport and keep you updated through to delivery.

FAQ

Questions buyers often ask

Do you send samples?

Yes. Tell us the product, format and delivery address. Samples ship with their technical data sheet.

Is every product available all year?

No. Each crop has its own harvest season. Some, such as allspice, are harvested only once a year, so we recommend booking ahead. See the harvest calendar on our home page.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product and format. Tell us the volume you need and we will explain how we can supply it.

Are your products organic?

Some lots come from certified organic producers and others from uncertified agroecological farms. We state the certification status of every lot in the quote.

Which documents will I receive?

Technical data sheet, phytosanitary and origin certificates, commercial invoice, packing list and transport document, plus the organic certificate for certified lots. If your country requires anything else, tell us.
