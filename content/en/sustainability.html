Sustainability

People and forest first, trade second.

Sustainability is not an add-on to our business. It is the reason for it: fair income for the producer families of Alta Verapaz, and a landscape of trees that stays standing.

Social sustainability

Income families can count on

Jobs at origin

Drying, fermenting, sorting and packing happen in Alta Verapaz.

Income all year

Several crops with different seasons spread family income across the calendar.

Environmental sustainability

Crops that need the trees

Everything we sell grows among trees. Cardamom grows in the shade of the canopy. Allspice, clove and cinnamon are trees themselves. Pepper climbs living trees. Cacao grows under shade.

That means the income from our products depends on keeping the trees, not on clearing them. Diversified plots also protect the soil, hold water and give a home to the region's wildlife.

When the forest is healthy, the crops do well. When the crops pay fairly, families have a reason to look after the forest.

Our commitments
  • Put producer wellbeing ahead of commercial gain.
  • Agree prices with producers before each harvest.
  • Buy through producer organizations.
  • Keep processing and its jobs at origin.
  • Favour agroforestry and shade-grown systems.
  • Identify the community of origin of every lot.
  • Report our progress to producers, partners and buyers.
