About us

A Guatemalan exporter with a social purpose.

KAB EXPORT exports spices and cacao from Alta Verapaz. We exist to build fair, lasting trade between the producer communities of the region and buyers in Canada and the United States.

Kaab
beehoneyearth
Our name

A Maya word for honey, and for the earth

We chose a Maya name because our products grow on Maya land. Kaab names the bee and its honey, and also the earth. To us it is a reminder of where everything we sell comes from, and of who we owe it to.

Who we are

Supply chain at origin, partners at destination

KAB EXPORT is a Guatemalan company. In Guatemala we organize the supply chain: we work with producer organizations, plan each harvest with them, and handle collection, preparation, documents and logistics.

In North America we work with commercial partners who know their markets and who share our view of how spices should be traded.

We put the producers, their communities and their environment first. A sale that does not work for the people who grew the product is not a sale we want.

Mission

Fair trade that starts with the producer

To connect the producer communities of Alta Verapaz with buyers in North America through fair, transparent and lasting trade, putting the wellbeing of producer families and the health of their land ahead of commercial gain.

Vision

Alta Verapaz as a trusted origin

For Alta Verapaz to be known in North America as a reliable origin of quality spices and cacao, where every purchase strengthens the communities and forests that produce them.

Values

How we work

“The people who grow a spice should be the first to benefit from it. Everything else we build starts there.”

Pedro Pablo comes from a family with several generations in agriculture. For three decades he has directed and structured companies in agribusiness, real estate and tourism in Guatemala and El Salvador, always with the same approach: patient capital, well-founded decisions and trust built over years, not in a single transaction.

He knows the spice business from the field. He runs an organic cardamom farm in El Salvador and has his own farm in Alta Verapaz. At KAB EXPORT he leads the supply chain at origin: the relationship with producer organizations, the planning of each harvest and the logistics to North America.

KAB EXPORT is part of DAConsortium, the business group for which he serves as Chief Revenue Architect.
