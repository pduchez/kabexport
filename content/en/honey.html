Organic honey

Apis mellifera · wildflower

Honey is where our name comes from: in Maya, kaab. We source it from apiaries surrounded by forest and wild vegetation, far from conventional farmland.

{{icon:honey}}

Raw, the way bees make it

Our honey comes from the wild blooms of Central America's forests and organic farmland. Each harvest carries the colour and flavour of its season's flowers.

We extract and filter it without heat, so it keeps its enzymes, aroma and natural properties. Over time it crystallises, which is a sign that the honey is raw and unprocessed.

Hives are managed without antibiotics or synthetic products, as organic production requires.

Common uses

  • Packing for retail brands
  • Food manufacturing: cereals, bars, drinks and sauces
  • Bakery and confectionery
  • Natural cosmetics
