Our name

In Maya, the word for honey also names the earth.

That is why we are called KAB. We work with what the Central American land gives and take it to the world with the same care it is grown with. Our three products share one ecosystem: the forest that shades the cardamom is where the bees gather nectar and where the vanilla orchid blooms.

The beeMakes the honey and pollinates the forest the other crops depend on.
The orchidVanilla is native to Mesoamerica. Its story began here.
The shadeCardamom grows under the trees, with no need to clear the forest.
