Vanilla

Vanilla planifolia

Vanilla is an orchid native to Mesoamerica. Its story began here, long before it reached kitchens around the world. We grow and cure it patiently, as it has been done for generations.

{{icon:vanilla}}

Months of work in every bean

Each vanilla flower opens for a single day and is pollinated by hand. The bean then takes around nine months to mature on the vine.

Curing begins after harvest: the beans are blanched, sweated, sun-dried and conditioned over several months. This slow process develops the vanillin and the more than one hundred aromatic compounds that make natural vanilla unique.

Grades

  • Gourmet (grade A): supple, glossy beans with higher moisture. For pastry, ice cream and direct use.
  • Extraction (grade B): drier beans with concentrated aroma. For extracts and industrial production.
