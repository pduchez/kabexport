Cacao · Fermented at origin

Fine-flavour cacao

Theobroma cacao

Cacao has been part of Maya life for thousands of years. In the Lachuá region, along the Chixoy river, and in Raxruhá, producer families and organizations grow it under shade and ferment and dry it in their own facilities.

{{icon:cacao}}

Value that stays where it grows

Fermentation and drying decide most of a cacao bean's flavour. When producers do that work themselves, in their own facilities, they keep more of the value of their harvest.

That is why we buy cacao already fermented and dried at origin, from organizations that have invested in processing, rather than buying wet beans to process elsewhere.

Cacao grows under shade trees, in plots that also hold timber, fruit and spices.

Common uses

  • Bean-to-bar and craft chocolate
  • Cocoa nibs
  • Cacao drinks
  • Confectionery and bakery
  • Cocoa butter and powder
