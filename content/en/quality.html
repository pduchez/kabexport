Quality

Quality is checked in every lot.

Paying producers fairly only works if the product meets what buyers need. We check quality at origin, lot by lot, and document it.

Our process

From the community to the container

  1. OriginThe community and producer organization of every lot is recorded.
  2. IntakeInspection and sampling when the product is collected.
  3. PreparationDrying, cleaning, sorting and grading in Alta Verapaz.
  4. AnalysisThe lot's key parameters are checked against the agreed specification.
  5. ReleaseOnly lots that meet the specification are shipped.

Organic certification

Stated clearly, lot by lot

Some of our producers hold organic certification and others farm with agroecological practices without being certified. We state the certification status of every lot in the quote.

We only sell a lot as organic when its certification covers the destination market: the Canada Organic Regime (COR) for Canada and the USDA National Organic Program (NOP) for the United States.

Documents with every shipment

  • Technical data sheet of the lot
  • Phytosanitary certificate
  • Certificate of origin
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Transport document (bill of lading or waybill)
  • Organic certificate, for certified lots
