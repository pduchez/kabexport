Organic certification

Documents for every market

Each market has its own rules for selling a product as organic. We provide the documents the destination country requires.

  • United States: USDA National Organic Program (NOP).
  • European Union (Germany and Spain): Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and Certificate of Inspection (COI).
  • Mexico: Organic Products Law (Ley de Productos Orgánicos).

Ask us for the current certificates for each product and we will send them with your quote.
